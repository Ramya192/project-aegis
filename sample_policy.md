# Corporate Travel Policy
**Document ID:** TRV-POL-2005-V3
**Policy Owner:** GCT-RM
**Effective Date:** 2026-02-01

## International Per Diems
Employees are entitled to a daily allowance based on destination.

| Destination | Daily Allowance |
|-------------|-----------------|
| USA         | $80             |
| Europe      | $70             |
| Asia        | $60             |

## Ground Transportation
Employees may expense taxi or rideshare costs up to $50 per trip.
Uber and Lyft are both acceptable platforms.
